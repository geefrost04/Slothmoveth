# Set 2 Diagram Prompts

Generate each image as a clean 4:3 landscape exam diagram on a white background. Use navy line art, one red accent only where needed, no logo, no mascot, no decorative frame, and no explanatory paragraphs. Keep labels in Thai where specified.

## Geometry Q1: Coordinate distance

Draw a Cartesian grid from -4 to 10 on x-axis and -2 to 12 on y-axis. Plot A(2,3) and B(8,11), draw a thin dashed right-angle construction showing horizontal 6 and vertical 8, and draw the diagonal AB. Labels only: A, B, 6, 8. No answer.

## Geometry Q5: Parallel-line angle

Draw two horizontal parallel lines labeled l1 and l2 with a diagonal transversal. Mark one pair of alternate interior angles: upper angle label (3x+15) degrees and lower angle label (5x-25) degrees. Use two subtle red arcs to show the matching angles. No answer.

## Geometry Q10: Square and inscribed circle

Draw a square with side label 10 cm. Draw the largest possible circle exactly inscribed in the square. Shade the region inside the square but outside the circle with pale gray dots. Label radius 5 cm with a thin line. No answer.

## Geometry Q17: Cube opposite faces

Draw one isometric cube. Put A on the top face and B on the front face. Label the hidden opposite-face reference as a small separate legend: opposite pairs A-F, B-E, C-D. Do not reveal an answer or highlight F on the cube.

## Geometry Q18: Pyramid cross-section

Draw a transparent square-based pyramid in simple navy line art. Add a red horizontal cutting plane parallel to the square base, halfway up the pyramid. Use a small label “ระนาบตัดขนานฐาน”. No answer.

## Geometry Q20: Square pyramid edges

Draw a square-based pyramid in navy line art. Clearly show the four base edges and four edges from apex to base corners. Use a dashed line only for one hidden base edge. No numeric answer, no title.
